Critical Weaknesses:
W1. The experiments do not convincingly establish that the motivating semantic distortion and semantic collapse are consequential failures of the baseline. The caption-set T2I gains are meaningful, but improvements over CLIP-FT are less pronounced elsewhere: Flickr30K caption-set I2T R@1 improves by only 0.3 points, and MSCOCO standard I2T/T2I R@1 improves by 2.0/0.7 points. Small gains alone do not invalidate the hypothesis, but the paper does not directly connect baseline retrieval errors to the proposed failure mechanisms. WSD/BSD describe average geometry without establishing whether meaningful attributes or relations were lost. Larger caption dispersion alone does not establish better semantic preservation, since it may also reflect variation unrelated to meaningful visual distinctions. Moreover, caption dispersion changes little when the alignment loss is removed. The current evidence therefore supports an effective training objective more clearly than it establishes and resolves the stated semantic failures.（缺少对语义失真和语义坍缩的直接验证）（可视化验证）
