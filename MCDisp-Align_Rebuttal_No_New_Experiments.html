R1 · W1(a)公式澄清 · 训练路径

Gradient paths for the low-rank factor U

对应质疑：U 不进入 Eq. 4、KL 或标准推理，并且在整个 Lalign 中缺少梯度，只能依赖最后阶段的子空间项。

Draft response

U does have a gradient path through the alignment objective, although it is absent from the matching score in Eq. 4, the diagonal KL term, and standard retrieval scoring. Specifically, the coverage term in Eq. 6 uses the full covariance A = Diag(vv) + UUT + εI. For one caption with an active coverage hinge, let δ = μt - μv and a = A-1δ. Its coverage-loss gradient is

∇ULcover = -(2β / D) aaTU.

This establishes a conditional training path beyond subspace alignment: the hinge must be active and aTU must be nonzero. In particular, this coverage gradient is zero at U = 0. Connectivity alone does not establish actual updates. We agree that standard retrieval uses only the learned centers, so the low-rank component is a training-time modeling component rather than part of that inference score. The gradient calculation establishes connectivity; it does not, by itself, establish the empirical contribution of U.

依据：paper.pdf 第4页 Eq. 1、第5页 Eq. 6 及 coverage 定义；标准检索说明见第5页 lines 260-262。

使用边界：此处按单条 caption 推导；对 K 条 caption 取平均时再平均相应梯度。hinge 激活不保证梯度一定非零。不能把这段写成“U 已被证明有用”，也不能用它回答 coverage 实际是否激活。

R1 · W1(b)公式澄清 · 梯度来源

Stop-gradient does not remove all text-variance gradients

对应质疑：sg 切断文本方差的对齐梯度，使其退化为 v0 = 0.04 附近的常数。

Draft response

We agree that stop-gradient blocks text-side gradients from the first three alignment terms. Our clarification concerns the full training objective; it does not refute that narrower observation about alignment. As stated in Section 2.3, matching and variance regularization update both modalities. In particular, moment matching gives

diag(ΣC) = (1 / K) Σkvkt + diag(Covk(μkt)),

so the caption-set variance in Eq. 4 depends on the individual text variances. Once the matching-to-variance gradient is fully enabled,

∂sij / ∂vj,k,dt = -sij / [2KD(1 + v̄jC)],

which is generally nonzero. Appendix A specifies that this matching gradient is blocked in the first two epochs, increased over epochs 3-4, and fully enabled at epoch 5. Thus the text variance is not structurally disconnected throughout training. We nevertheless agree that matching constrains aggregate variance and the regularizer favors v0; the existence of these gradients alone does not rule out a nearly constant solution or establish calibrated caption-level uncertainty.

依据：论文第4页 Eq. 2 / Eq. 4，第5页 lines 227-240、254-258，第12页 Appendix A。

使用边界：纠正的是“全程没有任何训练路径”的解读。审稿人关于文本方差近常数、是否具有语义意义的经验质疑仍需方差分布或其他已有证据；不要把“可获得梯度”替换成“实际学到了有效不确定性”。

R1 · W1(c)部分回应 · 仅解释数学形式

Dimension normalization of the coverage term

对应质疑：β[d²/D - 1]+ 在高维和正常特征尺度下始终为零。

Draft response

High dimensionality alone does not mathematically imply that the coverage hinge is zero. Let δ = μt - μv and A = Diag(vv) + UUT + εI. Dividing squared Mahalanobis distance by D provides a dimension-normalized scale: under the idealized residual model δ ∼ N(0, A),

E[δTA-1δ] = D.

However, caption centers need not follow this residual model, so this calculation is a scale rationale rather than a guarantee that the hinge is active for the learned representations. The actual activation depends on the residual and covariance scales. The formula alone therefore cannot establish how informative coverage is during training, and increasing β would not activate a hinge whose value is already zero.

依据：论文第5页 Eq. 6、lines 242-246；期望公式为条件明确的数学推导。

使用边界：不要声称“训练中 coverage 一直有效”，也不要把 d² ≤ D 描述为已经校准、具有固定置信概率的覆盖区间。若要正面回答“实际是否为零”，仍需已有日志中的激活率或 d²/D 统计。

R1 · W1(d)部分回应 · 承认尺度限制

Subspace weight versus covariance magnitude

对应质疑：子空间项的 γ 很小、只在最后阶段启用，且缺少 U 的尺度约束。

Draft response

The numerical value γ = 0.01 alone is insufficient to establish a negligible optimization effect, because the contribution depends on the loss gradients relative to those of the other terms. However, the scale concern is valid: the subspace term constrains orientation, not covariance magnitude. Rescaling U leaves its column space unchanged, and this term does not calibrate the eigenvalues of UUT. Section 2.3 explicitly states that it does not set the variance along each direction, and Appendix A specifies its late activation at epochs 9-10. We therefore distinguish the intended directional role from an empirically established contribution; neither the coefficient nor the objective definition alone establishes the latter.

依据：论文第5页 lines 248-255，第12页 γ 与启用日程。

使用边界：没有 loss / gradient 统计时，不要写“γ 小是因为已验证各损失尺度平衡”。还应核对实际 best checkpoint 轮次；若最终 checkpoint 早于第9轮，不能声称子空间项已对该模型产生训练贡献。

Draft response

The covariance-consistency concern is valid. Let c = (1/K) ?kvkt + diag(Covk(?kt)) be the caption-set marginal variance. The diagonal KL targets c with vv, while coverage uses A = Diag(vv) + UUT + ?I. Therefore,

diag(A) = vv + diag(UUT) + ?1.

If vv matches c, a nonzero low-rank factor adds marginal variance beyond that target. UUT cannot be interpreted as adding correlations only. Correct text-set moment matching does not resolve the discrepancy between the diagonal target and the full image covariance.

Since KL is a soft constraint, this does not prove that the learned model exactly counts the same variance twice; the objectives may trade off. Nevertheless, the formulation does not guarantee that A matches the caption-set marginal variance, and subspace alignment does not calibrate its magnitude. We therefore do not claim calibrated recovery of the caption-set covariance. Resolving this issue requires a revised covariance constraint and validation; no such tested revision is reported here.

R1 · W2协议澄清 · 归因问题未解决

Caption-set retrieval and standard retrieval are distinct protocols

对应质疑：五条描述预聚合不符合独立文本检索，且训练目标与集合评估直接耦合，可能偏向本文。

Draft response

The experiment in Section 3.2 evaluates image–set retrieval: each text-side item is a predefined set of five captions. All methods receive the same caption sets at evaluation, with CLIP aggregating its caption embeddings and probabilistic methods aggregating their caption representations. Image-side and text-side representations are computed independently. Standard image–text retrieval is evaluated separately in Section 3.3 using individual captions and cosine similarity between representation centers, without caption aggregation.

The set-level results therefore support the image–set task; they do not imply that test-time caption sets are available in every retrieval application. We agree that identical evaluation inputs alone do not isolate the effect of probabilistic alignment from caption-set training. The current comparison evaluates complete-framework performance under the specified protocol. It does not isolate dispersion modeling: that requires a deterministic baseline with matched caption-set supervision and training budget. Different objectives alone do not invalidate a framework comparison, but equal evaluation inputs do not establish controlled training conditions.

依据：论文第5页 lines 260-262、第6页 Section 3.2、第7页 Section 3.3。

使用边界：本段不否认集合任务依赖预定义分组，也不宣称“相同数据 = 训练公平”。“集合训练还是概率建模带来提升”仍需要匹配训练目标的 baseline 对照。与 R3-C1 对应，合并提交时可交叉引用。

Geometry does not directly establish semantic recovery

对应质疑：WSD/BSD 不能证明具体属性、关系丢失或恢复；增大离散度也可能来自无关变化；去掉 alignment 后 caption dispersion 几乎不变。

Draft response

WSD, BSD, and RSD characterize caption-space geometry; they do not directly identify which attributes or relations have been preserved. Improved retrieval and larger dispersion likewise do not, by themselves, establish a causal link between baseline errors and semantic distortion or collapse. Table 5 shows that average caption dispersion changes little when the alignment loss is removed, while Table 4 shows that retrieval improves with the loss.

These observations support the narrower conclusion that, in the reported ablation, alignment improves retrieval without materially reducing average within-set caption dispersion. They do not directly demonstrate recovery of specific lost semantic distinctions. We agree that the interpretation should distinguish this empirical observation from the broader semantic mechanism motivating the method.

依据：论文第7-8页 Section 3.4、Table 3、第8页 Tables 4-5。

使用边界：这是一段证据边界回应，不是对“请直接展示错误和语义恢复案例”的完整回答。若保留恢复特定语义的强结论，仍需相应案例、属性/关系分析或其他直接证据。

Multiple captions do not guarantee complementary semantic coverage

Draft response

The five-caption structure of MSCOCO and Flickr30K provides multiple descriptions of each image, but caption count alone does not establish complementary semantic coverage. Figure 1 illustrates differences in descriptive emphasis; it is not a quantitative validation of substantial semantic diversity. The reported results evaluate the benchmarks in aggregate and do not establish how gains vary with caption-set semantic diversity, complementary objects, attributes, or relations.

Our present evidence is therefore limited to the tested caption-set distributions. We acknowledge that it does not establish the requested diversity-conditioned advantage, image-distribution coverage of distinct meanings, or hard-negative separation. These remain limits of the current validation rather than conclusions that can be inferred from having five captions per image.

依据：论文第2页 Figure 1、第6-8页现有评估内容。

Does CLIP-FT use the same caption-set training objective?

对应质疑：CLIP-FT 的训练目标是否相同？提升来自 dispersion 建模还是集合训练？

提交前必须核对：下面英文依据当前仓库实现撰写。当前代码不能自动证明论文所报 checkpoint 使用了同一版本及配置。确认投稿模型确实采用“每图随机一条 caption + 标准双向 CE”后，才能使用其中关于原实验训练方式的陈述。
Conditional draft response

CLIP-FT and MCDisp-Align do not use identical training objectives. CLIP-FT uses the standard bidirectional image–caption contrastive objective, with one caption sampled for each image at each training step; MCDisp-Align uses caption-set matching together with its alignment terms. Sharing an image pool and caption annotations does not make these objectives equivalent.

The existing comparison measures the benefit of the complete framework over pairwise fine-tuning, but it does not separately quantify the contributions of set training and distribution modeling. We agree that the training objectives need to be stated explicitly and that the current comparison does not establish that the gains arise from dispersion modeling alone.
